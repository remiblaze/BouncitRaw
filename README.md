# Bouncit Raw — one-knob stereo bouncing echo

![Bouncit Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/BouncitRaw/main/bouncitraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**Instant stereo ping-pong delay in a single knob.**

Bouncit Raw is the free one-knob version of Bouncit. Turn one dial and your sound starts bouncing left-to-right in a tempo-synced echo — no setup, no menus.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/BouncitRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`BouncitRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **ECHO** knob. Turn it up and it opens the whole echo at once — more feedback repeats, more wet blend, and a warmer, darker tail — all front-loaded and level-matched so it grows wider and deeper without a volume jump.

The delay time is locked to a tempo-synced quarter-note bounce with a stereo swing, so the repeats ping-pong left-to-right in time with your project. A gentle tape-style saturator and filtered feedback keep the tails musical. Works on synths, drums, vocals, and full mixes.
