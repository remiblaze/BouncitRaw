# Bouncit Raw: one-knob stereo bouncing echo

![Bouncit Raw free one-knob stereo echo plugin UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/BouncitRaw/main/bouncitraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**Instant stereo ping-pong delay in a single knob.**

Bouncit Raw is the free one-knob version of Bouncit. Turn one dial and your sound starts bouncing left-to-right in a tempo-synced echo, no setup, no menus.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/BouncitRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`BouncitRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Bouncit Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`BouncitRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Bouncit Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **ECHO** knob. Turn it up and it opens the whole echo at once, more feedback repeats, more wet blend, and a warmer, darker tail, all front-loaded and level-matched so it grows wider and deeper without a volume jump.

The delay time is locked to a tempo-synced quarter-note bounce with a stereo swing, so the repeats ping-pong left-to-right in time with your project. A gentle tape-style saturator and filtered feedback keep the tails musical. Works on synths, drums, vocals, and full mixes.
